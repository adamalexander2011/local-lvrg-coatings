How long does setup take?

We confirm your schedule after reviewing your information and access. Text messaging needs carrier approval, so setup and live texting can have different dates. We keep you updated in writing.

What should I have ready?

Your website and business details; the services and cities you cover; estimate availability; approved answers to common questions; and your notification contact. If you add ads, we also need your ad-account access and approved photos. Any additional business-verification details are collected during setup.

Text-based service only. Our team does not manually call your leads. Questions that need your judgment are passed back to you.

Questions owners actually ask

Does anybody call my leads?

No. Local LVRG follows up through text-based conversations. There is no voice bot or manual calling team. You or your team handle anything that needs a personal call, the on-site estimate and the sale.

What am I paying for each month?

A managed text follow-up and estimate-booking service configured around your business. The Estimate Catcher is $297/month, with the $500 setup waived, US text delivery covered and $50 in AI conversation credit included with each verified monthly payment. You provide the business rules; we manage the system.

Is Booked Solid $997 on top of the $297?

No. The $997 monthly service includes the Estimate Catcher, managed advertising and the landing page. Ad spend is separate, with a $1,500 monthly minimum paid directly to the platforms. Budget at least $2,497/month in total.

Do I need paid ads to start?

No. If you already get inquiries, start with the Estimate Catcher. Booked Solid adds managed ads when you want new inquiry sources and have the budget and calendar capacity for them. The follow-up system is prepared before ads launch.

Will it give customers prices or make promises?

Only using pricing and answers you approve. Otherwise it explains the next step and offers an estimate. Unusual questions are passed to you with the conversation context. If asked whether it is AI, it answers honestly.

Can I keep my office staff and existing website?

Yes. We configure the service around your team's role and availability, including after-hours coverage. If your website can capture and connect inquiries, we use it. Any extra website work is discussed separately.

Can you follow up with old inquiries?

Yes, when the records and permission to contact those people are appropriate. Existing opt-outs are respected. We review that source before connecting it.

When will my system be live?

After we receive the required business information and access, complete setup, secure the required messaging approvals and verify the test conversation, booking and notification. Your launch date is confirmed in writing. See the setup steps.
