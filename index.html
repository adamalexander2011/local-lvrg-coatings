What should I have ready?

Your business details and website; services and cities you cover; estimate availability; approved answers to common questions; and your notification contact. For Booked Solid, also have your ad-account access and approved project photos ready. We collect any additional verification details during setup.

Before we get started.

Do you call the leads?

No. The standard service is text-based. We respond to inquiries, ask project questions, follow up and book estimate requests. When a homeowner needs a phone conversation, we organize their details and identify who needs a call and why. Your team handles that call. Phone follow-up may be scoped separately based on your requirements and availability.

Do I have to manage the software?

No. This is a managed service. We set up and manage the process. You receive estimate requests, lead information and a clear notification when something needs your attention.

What is behind the text conversations?

We use AI for text conversations, configured specifically to represent your company. It works from your services, project requirements, service area, approved answers and booking rules. We build and manage that setup for you. We do not use voice AI. Questions outside your approved information and rules go to your team.

How much should I plan to spend on advertising?

For a full-service campaign, plan on approximately $1,300–$1,500 per month in advertising spend, usually paced at roughly $43–$50 per day. Advertising spend is separate from the $997 management fee and is paid directly to the advertising platform.

Is the $297 service an extra charge?

No. Booked Solid includes the Estimate Catcher service within the $997 monthly management fee. The $297 option is for companies that only need their existing inquiries followed up and do not need advertising management.

What makes an inquiry ready for an estimate?

We use the criteria agreed with you, such as project type, service area, floor condition and timing. We collect the relevant details and offer the estimate times you make available. Anything outside those rules or needing a personal conversation is handed back to your team. A booked request is not a guaranteed sale.

Do you give homeowners prices or close the job?

No. Your team handles pricing, quotes, on-site estimates and sales conversations. We collect project information and help the homeowner take the next step.

Can I keep my website and office staff?

Yes. We agree on the connected lead sources and who handles each next step during setup. Booked Solid includes a campaign landing page. Any additional website work is discussed separately.

When will the service be live?

After we have the required business details and access, complete setup and approvals, and test the conversation, booking and notification. We confirm your launch schedule in writing. See the setup steps.
